Lock-out / tag-out steps

  1. Tell everyone nearby that you are isolating the circuit.
  2. Switch off and open the supply at the main isolator.
  3. Fit your own padlock and a tag with your name and date.
  4. Test the tester on a known live source.
  5. Test the circuit is dead, phase to phase and phase to earth.
  6. Test the tester again, then start work.

Only the person who fitted the lock removes it.
